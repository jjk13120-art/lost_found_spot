Privacy Policy

At Lost & Found Spot, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use our services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal details such as name, email, and contact number when you sign up.
  • Data you submit in reports (item description, location, etc.).
  • Technical data like IP address and browser type for security and analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To facilitate connecting lost and found items.
  • To notify you about account activity.
  • To improve site performance and security.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We only share data when:

  • Legally required by authorities.
  • Necessary to protect the safety of users and the platform.

4. Data Security

We implement strict measures to protect your data. However, no online service can guarantee 100% security.

5. Your Rights

You can request account deletion or data removal by contacting us.

6. Updates to Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. The latest version will always be available on this page.
